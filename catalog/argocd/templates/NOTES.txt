Onboard a service to GitOps:
  1. Chart lives in catalog/<name>/ with the six labels.
  2. Add catalog/<name>/argo.yaml:  {name: <helm release name>, namespace: <ns>}
  3. Commit. The ApplicationSet generates the Application; auto-sync applies it.
  Remove the argo.yaml (or the chart) to offboard — with gitops.prune=true
  the resources are removed too.
